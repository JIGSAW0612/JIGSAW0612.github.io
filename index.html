I am an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Electronic Engineering at Sogang University, advised by Prof. Suk-Ju Kang in the VIDS Lab. I received my B.S. in Computer Engineering from Hansung University.

My research is on efficient AI: making large multimodal and generative models fast enough to use in practice. My recent work, SparSTAR, is a training-free sparse attention method for autoregressive video generation. I am also interested in multimodal models and embodied AI.

SparSTAR: Sparse Attention for SpaceTime AutoRegressive Video Synthesis

Jongbeom Lee*, Hyunwoo Yu*, Jincheol Yang, Jaemin Choi, Suk-Ju Kang†

A training-free block-sparse attention method for video visual autoregressive generation. At each expensive scale, it builds a fresh per-head mask instead of reusing one from an earlier scale or clip, with low selector overhead.
