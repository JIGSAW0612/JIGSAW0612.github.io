arXiv 2026

OpenAttention: One Percent Is Enough with Tail Proxies

Hyunwoo Yu, Jongbeom Lee, Hyunbin Kim, Sungjun Park, Jaemin Choi, Jincheol Yang, Seunghun Moon, Bongjoon Hyun, Kyomin Sohn, Kyeongbo Kong, Suk-Ju Kang

Sparse attention that folds the unread tail of the KV cache into a few count-aware cluster proxies, with an ErrorGate that spends the exact-read budget on the clusters with the largest estimated error. Keeps 99% of dense accuracy at a 1% read on RULER-32K HARD with Llama-3.1-8B, and its kernel runs decode attention 10× faster than dense at a 1% read.

arXiv 2026

SparSTAR: Sparse Attention for SpaceTime AutoRegressive Video Synthesis

Jongbeom Lee*, Hyunwoo Yu*, Jincheol Yang, Jaemin Choi, Suk-Ju Kang†

A training-free block-sparse attention method for video visual autoregressive generation. At each expensive scale, it builds a fresh per-head mask instead of reusing one from an earlier scale or clip, with low selector overhead.

ICML 2026 Workshop on Resource-Adaptive Foundation Model Inference (AdaptFM)

Reducing Attention Distribution Error with Unified Tail Aggregation for Sparse Attention

Hyunwoo Yu*, Jongbeom Lee*, Jaemin Choi*, Jincheol Yang*, Yubin Cho*, Joonsik Nam, Seunghun Moon, Jung-Woo Chang, Bongjoon Hyun, Kyomin Sohn, Kyeongbo Kong, Suk-Ju Kang†

Shows that the Top-K oracle used to evaluate sparse attention in video diffusion transformers cuts off the tail of the attention distribution, and proposes a low-delta oracle policy with Unified Tail Aggregation, which adds one aggregated logit to restore it. Up to 97.4% lower MSE than the Top-K oracle at 50% sparsity.
